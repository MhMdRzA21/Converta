Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Files you upload

A file you select for conversion is temporarily uploaded to the server, converted, handed back to you for download, and then — both the original and the converted file — deleted right away. No copy of your file is kept, archived, or shared with any third party. The content of your file isn't read or analyzed; it just passes through the conversion software.

What information is collected

Like most sites, the server automatically keeps a small amount of technical information — IP address, browser type, request time — in security logs for a short period. This is used only to prevent abuse (like an unusual volume of automated requests) and isn't linked to the content of your files.

Ads and cookies

This site may show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve more relevant ads. You can manage Google's ad personalization settings here.

Your rights

Since no files are retained, there's nothing to request deletion of. If you have any questions about how the site works, reach out through the contact page.
