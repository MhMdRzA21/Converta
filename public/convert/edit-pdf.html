      Frequently asked questions

      Why isn't this a direct in-browser PDF text editor?

      PDF doesn't store text as paragraphs the way Word does — it records exact character positions on a page, with no concept of a paragraph reflowing. Tools that let you click and retype text directly in a PDF are really just covering the old text and drawing new text in roughly the same spot; if your replacement is longer or shorter, it won't reflow correctly. Converting to Word and back gives you real reflow because Word actually has a layout engine that PDF doesn't.

      Will my formatting survive the round trip?

      For standard documents — reports, letters, forms — yes, formatting carries through both conversions reliably. Complex multi-column layouts or heavily designed PDFs may need a quick check after converting back.

      Does this work with non-English or right-to-left text?

      Yes — PDF to Word conversion here uses a layout-aware engine built to correctly reconstruct complex scripts (Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, and others) and real tables, rather than the disconnected, incorrectly-ordered letters that generic PDF import tools often produce.
