    Frequently asked questions

    Will my fonts and formatting be preserved?

    Yes — the conversion uses the same rendering engine as desktop office software, so fonts, spacing, and layout carry over as they appeared in the original document.

    Does this work with older .doc files?

    Yes, both the older .doc format and the newer .docx format are supported, along with OpenDocument .odt files.

    Is there a watermark on the PDF?

    No — the output PDF has no watermark or branding added.
