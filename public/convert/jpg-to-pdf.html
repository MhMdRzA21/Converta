    Drop in one image or several — drag and drop, or click to browse your files.

    3. Download

    Frequently asked questions

    Does converting JPG to PDF reduce image quality?

    No — the image is embedded in the PDF at its original resolution, so there's no extra compression or quality loss beyond the JPG's own.

    Can I combine multiple JPGs into one PDF?

    Yes. Select or drop more than one image and they'll be combined into a single multi-page PDF, in the order you added them.

    Is there a file size limit?

    Each image can be up to 25MB, and you can add up to 20 images per conversion.
